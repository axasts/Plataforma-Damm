This page explains how to delete your Aurabattle account and the personal data associated with it. Aurabattle is operated by Alex Arias Tomas.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the address linked to your account to:

axasts98@gmail.com

Use the subject "Delete my Aurabattle account" and include your username. We will verify that the request comes from the account owner and process it within 30 days. We will email you to confirm once it is done.

Your battle videos, chat messages, comments and votesYes — deleted or anonymised

What we may keep

We may retain a limited amount of data where the law requires it, or where it is needed to handle safety reports, fraud or abuse. Any such data is kept only for as long as necessary and is not used for any other purpose. Aggregated or anonymised data that can no longer identify you may be retained.

Retention

Once your deletion request is verified, your personal data is removed or anonymised within 30 days, except for the limited categories described above.
