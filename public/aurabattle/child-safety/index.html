Child Safety Standards

Aurabattle · CSAE / CSAM standards · Last updated: 5 September 2026

Aurabattle (operated by Alex Arias Tomas) has a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our published standards against CSAE, as required by Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy, and how anyone can report a concern.

Our commitment

  • We prohibit any content, conduct, or contact on the Service that sexualises, exploits, endangers, or abuses a minor.
  • We prohibit CSAM in any form, and any attempt to produce, share, request, or promote it.
  • We prohibit grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and the solicitation of minors.
  • Violations lead to immediate content removal and account termination, and, where CSAM is involved, preservation of evidence and reporting to the competent authorities.

Minimum age & protections for minors

Aurabattle is for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly allow children under 13. Users under 16 receive additional protections: they are never displayed as map-pin owners under their name, and a stricter chat filter applies to their interactions.

How to report a child-safety concern

Anyone can report CSAE, CSAM, or any child-safety concern:

  • In the app: every piece of content and every user profile has a Report and a Block control. Reports about child safety are prioritised.
  • By email: axasts98@gmail.com — our designated child-safety contact.

How we handle reports

  • We review child-safety reports promptly and remove violating content.
  • We terminate the accounts of users who violate this policy.
  • Where CSAM is identified, we preserve the relevant evidence and report it to the appropriate authorities and recognised bodies (for example, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local law enforcement), in line with applicable law.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement and comply with all relevant child-safety laws in the jurisdictions where we operate.

Designated contact

Our designated point of contact for child-safety matters is Alex Arias Tomas, reachable at axasts98@gmail.com. This person is prepared to discuss our CSAE prevention and compliance practices.
