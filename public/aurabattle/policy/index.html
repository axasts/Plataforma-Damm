Privacy Policy

Aurabattle · Last updated: 5 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alex Arias Tomas ("Aurabattle", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use the Aurabattle mobile app and website at aurabattle.pro (the "Service"). The data controller is Alex Arias Tomas. For any privacy question or request, contact us at axasts98@gmail.com.

Who can use Aurabattle

Aurabattle is for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Users under 16 have additional protections (they are never shown as map-pin owners under their name, and have a stricter chat filter).

How we use your data

  • To provide the Service: accounts, battles, live streams, voting, rankings, the map, chat and comments.
  • To keep users safe: moderation, handling reports, blocking, and enforcing our rules.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To understand and improve how the app is used.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

Service providers (processors)

We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf, under contract:

  • Supabase — accounts, database, storage (auth, profiles, avatars).
  • Mux — hosting and playback of recorded battle videos.
  • LiveKit — real-time live-stream transport.
  • Expo — push notification delivery.
  • Google and Apple — sign-in, if you choose to use social login.

Some providers may process data outside the EU; where they do, appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) apply.

Content visibility & downloads

Battles you take part in or film are public content within the Service and can be watched, voted on and commented on by others. Battle clips carry an aurabattle.pro watermark and may be downloaded by users to make edits and share them elsewhere. Do not film or publish content you are not comfortable being public.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can request to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent (e.g. turn off location) at any time. To exercise these rights, contact axasts98@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

Data retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. Live chat messages are kept up to 90 days then archived. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep some data to comply with the law or handle safety reports.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and update the date above; significant changes will be notified in the app.
